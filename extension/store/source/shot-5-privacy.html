OpenApply

Reads a page only when you click the icon. No background scripts, no access until you ask.

Just two permissions: the active tab, and reading it, on click. No host access to every site.

No account needed to install. Sign up only when you want to save your first job.

Open source. Read every line at github.com/SergeyKhval/OpenApply.
