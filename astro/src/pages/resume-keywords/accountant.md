We read two accountant postings live in September 2026, a staff accountant role at a robotics company (5+ years, audit support focus) and a senior accountant role at a satellite manufacturer (3+ years, GAAP focus), and checked the pattern against the broader current market for staff and senior accounting roles. Both wanted the same core: full-cycle month-end close, GAAP fluency, and Excel skills specific enough to name, not just "proficient in Excel."

## Must-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Progressive accounting experience (3 to 5+ years) | Required in both | State your years and whether they were progressive (increasing scope), not just tenure. |
| GAAP knowledge, applied in practice | Required in both | Name a specific area you applied it to: revenue recognition, accruals, or another. |
| Full-cycle month-end close | Required in both | Describe your role in close: journal entries, reconciliations, variance analysis, and the timeline you hit. |
| General ledger accounting and account reconciliations | Required in both | Name the accounts you reconciled (cash, AR, AP, prepaids) and how you resolved discrepancies. |
| Bachelor's degree in accounting, finance, or related field | Required in both | List your degree plainly; if you don't have one, lead with CPA progress or equivalent experience instead. |
| Excel skills named specifically (VLOOKUP, pivot tables, complex analyses) | Required in both, phrased with specific functions | Name the functions you actually use, matching the posting's own wording. |

## Nice-to-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CPA license or actively CPA-track | Preferred in both, required outright at more senior levels | State your exact status: licensed, sitting for exams, or credit hours completed. |
| Experience supporting financial audits | Required at one company, preferred broadly | Name the audit type (external, internal) and your specific role in it. |
| NetSuite or a comparable accounting system | Preferred in both | Only list systems you've used hands-on, not just "ERP experience." |
| Public accounting background | A plus, especially with Big 4 or regional firm experience | Name the firm type and what you audited or prepared there. |
| Consolidations or multi-entity accounting | A plus at companies with complex structures | Describe the entity structure you worked across, if true. |

## How to use this without keyword-stuffing

"GAAP" and "month-end close" appear on almost every accountant resume already; what separates candidates is naming the specific accounts, systems, and close timeline you actually worked with. Use the must-haves above to check your bullets are specific enough, then paste the real posting in below with your resume for a check against that exact role.
