We read four UX/product designer postings live in September 2026, at an ecommerce and health-benefits company, a fintech SaaS company, a governance SaaS company, and a health-tech hardware company, ranging from 3+ to 5+ years. Nearly all of them wanted the same three things up front: real Figma depth (not "familiar with"), a portfolio that shows the full process, and comfort designing data-heavy B2B products, not just marketing sites.

## Must-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Figma, in depth (components, variants, auto layout, prototyping) | Required in all 4 | Name the specific capabilities you use daily, not just "Figma." |
| A portfolio showing end-to-end process | Required in all 4 | Link it directly; make sure it shows discovery through delivery, not just final screens. |
| 3 to 5+ years of UX/UI or product design experience | Required, matched to seniority | State your years and the product type (B2B SaaS, ecommerce, dashboard) to match the posting. |
| B2B, SaaS, or data-rich product experience | Required in 3 of 4 | Name a specific complex workflow or data-heavy screen you designed. |
| Design systems familiarity | Required in 3 of 4 | Say whether you built, scaled, or just used a design system, and which one. |
| Cross-functional communication and collaboration | Required in all 4 | Point to a decision you influenced by presenting a design rationale. |

## Nice-to-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Generative AI tool fluency (e.g. Claude, Gemini) or designing AI-powered features | Explicit in 2 of 4, a fast-growing ask | Name the tool and what you used it for: ideation, copy drafting, or the AI feature itself. |
| Accessibility standards (WCAG) | Explicit at the ecommerce company | Name a specific accessibility fix or audit you did, not just "accessibility-minded." |
| HTML/CSS or dev handoff literacy | Preferred at 2 of 4 | Only claim it if you can actually read a diff or discuss a CSS constraint with engineers. |
| Owning user research (not just consuming it) | Preferred at 2 of 4 | Name a research method you ran yourself and a design decision it changed. |
| Scaling an enterprise design system | Preferred at the governance SaaS company | Describe your specific contribution, not just "worked in a design system." |

## How to use this without keyword-stuffing

A portfolio link does more work than any keyword on this list, so don't let the resume text oversell what the portfolio can't back up. Use the keywords above to decide which 2-3 case studies to lead with for a given posting; then paste the real job description in below with your resume for a check against that specific role's requirements.
