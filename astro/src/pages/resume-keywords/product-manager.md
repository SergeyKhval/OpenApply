We read product manager postings live in September 2026 at a B2B commerce marketplace and a MarTech SaaS company, both asking for 4 to 6 years of PM experience, and cross-checked the pattern against a broader set of current PM listings. The two postings differed in domain but asked for almost the same underlying skill set: ship things end to end, manage stakeholders who don't report to you, and decide with incomplete data.

## Must-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 4+ years of Product Management experience (SaaS, marketplace, or B2B) | Required in both postings | State your years and the type of product (B2B, marketplace, platform), matched to the posting. |
| Shipped a customer-facing product from concept to scale | Required, described as a track record not a one-off | Name one product or feature you took from idea to shipped, with a result. |
| Cross-functional stakeholder management (design, engineering, GTM) | Required | Name the functions you worked across and a disagreement you resolved. |
| Data-driven prioritization | Required | Cite a metric that changed your roadmap, not just "data-driven." |
| Comfort operating in ambiguity | Required, phrased as "navigating unclear situations" | Describe a time the problem wasn't well-defined yet and how you scoped it. |
| Written and verbal communication | Required | Point to a doc (PRD, strategy memo) that changed a decision. |

## Nice-to-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Technical background or CS degree | Preferred at the more technical company | List relevant technical experience even if your title wasn't "engineer." |
| Comfort using AI tools in everyday product work (research, docs, analysis) | An explicit ask at one company, likely to spread | Name the tool and task, e.g. using an LLM to synthesize user interviews, not "AI-forward." |
| Shipping or improving AI-powered product features | Emerging preference | Describe the feature and what made it AI-powered versus a rules-based version. |
| Marketplace, payments, or logistics domain experience | Preferred where relevant to the product | Only claim the domain if you can speak to its specific constraints. |
| Experience in a regulated or compliance-heavy industry | A plus at the company that needed it | Name the regulation or constraint you actually worked within. |

## How to use this without keyword-stuffing

PM postings reward specificity over vocabulary. "Cross-functional stakeholder management" on its own means nothing to a reviewer; the same words attached to "aligned support, engineering, and sales on a pricing change that cut churn 8%" mean everything. Once you have the actual posting for a role you want, paste it in below with your resume to see exactly which of its requirements you've already proven and which ones you haven't written down yet.
