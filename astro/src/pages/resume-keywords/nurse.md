We read RN postings live in September 2026 across an outpatient surgery center, a behavioral health facility, and a concierge emergency clinic. Unlike office-job postings, RN listings are fairly standardized: licensure and core certifications are non-negotiable, and the differences show up in specialty-specific experience and the EMR system a facility uses.

## Must-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Active, unencumbered RN license in the state where the job is | Required everywhere we looked | List your license number's state(s) and current status directly, don't make a reviewer guess. |
| Graduate of an accredited nursing program | Required everywhere | Name your degree type (ADN, BSN, diploma) and school. |
| BLS/CPR certification, current | Required everywhere | List the certifying body and expiration; an expired cert reads as a red flag. |
| Specialty-specific clinical experience, when the role has one (e.g. OR circulating, med-surg, behavioral health) | Required whenever the posting names a unit | State your years in that specific unit, not just total nursing years. |
| EMR/computer proficiency | Required or clearly expected in most postings | Name the actual system(s) you've used (Epic, Cerner, KIPU, Amkai, or others). |

## Nice-to-have keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| BSN (Bachelor of Science in Nursing) | Preferred over ADN/diploma at several facilities | List it if you have it; if you're ADN with a BSN in progress, say so. |
| ACLS certification | Preferred broadly, often required outright in ICU/ER settings | List current status; note if you're within any grace period a specialty requires. |
| Facility-specific software (e.g. Amkai Office/Charts, KIPU, or similar) | Requested where the facility already uses it | Only list systems you've actually worked in. |
| Strong technical nursing skills named in the posting (e.g. IV starts, wound care) | Called out explicitly in some postings | Match the posting's own wording where it's true of you; don't rely on synonyms an ATS might miss. |
| Soft skills: communication, multitasking, professional boundaries | Present in most postings, rarely gating on their own | Attach these to a real example rather than listing them bare. |

## How to use this without keyword-stuffing

For clinical roles, the honest overlap matters more than anywhere else on this list: a missing license or expired certification isn't something a well-written resume can talk around, and claiming a specialty skill you don't have creates real risk once you're on shift. Use this list to make sure your license, certifications, and specialty experience are stated clearly and exactly as the posting names them, then paste the real posting in below with your resume to see what's actually missing.
