"Marketing Manager" covers two quite different jobs, and we saw both in postings live in September 2026: a demand-generation manager at a B2B SaaS company asking for 5+ years of pipeline-driving campaign work, and a social media lead at an HR-tech company asking for 5-7 years of brand storytelling and community management. If you're applying broadly, read which flavor a specific posting actually wants before you lead with the wrong projects.

## Must-have keywords (both flavors)

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5+ years of relevant marketing experience | Required in both postings | State your years and which flavor (demand gen, brand/social, field) matches the role. |
| A track record of owned campaigns, not just support | Required | Name a specific campaign you owned end to end and its result. |
| Cross-functional partnership (sales, executives, or creative) | Required in both, with different partners | Name who you partnered with and what you delivered together. |
| Data-informed decisions | Required | Cite one metric a campaign was judged on and what you did when it missed. |
| Comfort in fast-paced, ambiguous environments | Required | Describe a shifting-priorities situation and how you reprioritized. |

## Nice-to-have and flavor-specific keywords

| Keyword | What we saw | How to prove it on your resume |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Marketing automation and CRM (Salesforce, Marketo) | Required for demand-gen roles specifically | Name the platform and one workflow you built or ran in it, not just "used Salesforce." |
| ABM (account-based marketing) motions | Required for demand-gen, 1:Few and 1:Many mentioned specifically | Describe a real ABM campaign and its target segment. |
| Social listening and community tools (Sprout Social, Meltwater) | Required for brand/social roles specifically | Name the tool and what you monitored or acted on with it. |
| A portfolio of platform-native content | Preferred for brand/social roles | Link real examples, not a generic case study. |
| AI or agentic workflow tools (e.g. Glean, Clay, or similar) | An emerging plus, not yet standard | Name the specific tool and task, e.g. account research or content drafting. |

## How to use this without keyword-stuffing

Don't try to be both flavors on one resume. If the posting wants demand generation, lead with pipeline numbers and CRM ownership; if it wants brand and social, lead with content you made and communities you grew. Once you have the actual job description, paste it in below with your resume to see which of its specific requirements you've already covered.
